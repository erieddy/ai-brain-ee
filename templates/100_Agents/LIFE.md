# LIFE.md — Weekly Life Report Engine

**Answers:** what's happening at home, with family, and in the rest of the user's life?
**Mode:** `personal` · **Runs:** Sundays · **Window:** Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59
**Template:** `90_Templates/Life_Report.md` · **Output:** `20_Areas/22_Personal/Weekly_Summaries/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (the Monday)

## One Audience

**There is no shareable half.** Not sent, pasted, or summarized to anyone. No reason to soften anything.

The week runs through Sunday because the weekend is when most personal life happens.

## Steps

### 0. Preflight (soft)

An unswept Inbox is **not** a stop. Personal items often sit raw, and surfacing them is this report's job. Read the raw captures and say so.

### 0b. Coverage check

Count what exists before writing: personal projects, personal meetings, personal captures this week, personal contacts, contacts with birthdays.

> [!DANGER] A report built on an empty vault reads as "everything's fine"
> That's a fabricated conclusion. **If coverage is thin, the thinness is the headline.**

### 1. 30-day radar

Birthdays, anniversaries, trips, appointments, deadlines in the next 30 days.

- Use the stored month and day, ignore the stored year, roll forward if passed. State the age or year count
- 🔴 anything inside 7 days, repeated at the top
- Every row has **Prep needed** and a **Source**
- **Never invent a date.** Look for collisions between work travel and family dates

### 2. Sections

- **🏡 Home & Family:** what happened, what's now open
- **⚠️ Slipping & Stalled:** personal commitments open 3+ days; personal projects quiet 14+ days
- **🕳️ Blind Spots:** missing records, empty fields that break a check, patterns in 2+ sources. State the consequence
- **⚖️ Work/Life Boundary Check:** measure the week against the boundaries in `USER.md`. Read work facts **from the vault only**; this instance can't see the work calendar
- **🎯 The One Thing**
- **📇 Data Coverage:** the counts from step 0b

### 3. Save and log

Save from the template, log in `101_long_term_memory/`, put dated commitments in the personal tracker.
