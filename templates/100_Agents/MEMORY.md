## How This File Is Kept (read before editing)

- **People get name, relationship, and key date. Nothing else**
- **No roster** (that's `TEAM.md`) and **no standing rules** (that's `AGENTS.md`)
- **Resolved means deleted**, not marked resolved
- **Prune every time you touch it**
- **Verify against the source note before reporting a fact from here.** Summaries rot
